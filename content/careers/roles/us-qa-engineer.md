## About the Role

Skill Stand In works with AI training and evaluation opportunities available through platforms such as DataAnnotation, Handshake AI, and Alignerr.

These platforms provide projects designed to improve AI systems through human evaluation and feedback. Projects may involve reviewing AI-generated responses, evaluating software-testing approaches, identifying errors or missing scenarios, comparing responses, providing human-written feedback, and creating rubrics that define what a high-quality response should include.

As a QA Engineer with Skill Stand In, your primary responsibility is to set up, maintain, and manage the assigned platform accounts and coordinate the work associated with them.

## Responsibilities

- Create and complete onboarding for assigned AI training platforms.
- Complete required profile, identity verification, and account setup steps.
- Maintain accurate professional and account information.
- Monitor assigned accounts for available projects, tasks, notifications, and platform updates.
- Complete account-holder-specific actions when required by the platform.
- Monitor project activity and account status.
- Track earnings, payments, and payout activity.
- Maintain organized records related to assigned accounts and projects.
- Coordinate with Skill Stand In and assigned experts regarding available work and project requirements.
- Report account, technical, payment, verification, or compliance issues promptly.
- Maintain the dedicated work laptop and required browser profiles for assigned platforms.
- Follow Skill Stand In procedures as well as the applicable rules and requirements of each platform.

## Qualifications

- Professional experience as a QA Engineer, Software Test Engineer, SDET, QA Analyst, or similar role.
- Hands-on experience testing real production applications.
- Experience creating and executing test plans, test cases, and regression testing.
- Experience identifying, reproducing, documenting, and verifying software defects.
- Understanding of functional, integration, regression, API, browser, and end-to-end testing.
- Familiarity with tools such as Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, Postman, browser developer tools, or similar technologies.
- Experience with test automation is preferred.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
- Strong written English and attention to detail.
- Ability to work independently and reliably manage assigned accounts and responsibilities.

## Time Commitment

Approximately 5–10 hours per week, depending on platform activity and available projects.

## Onboarding Payment

You will receive a one-time $100 onboarding payment after successfully completing the required initial platform onboarding process.

## Dedicated Work Laptop

After successful onboarding, Skill Stand In will provide you with a dedicated work laptop for the role.

The assigned platform accounts will be set up in dedicated browser profiles on this laptop. It is intended exclusively for Skill Stand In work and should not contain personal files, personal accounts, saved personal passwords, banking information, identity documents, or other private information.

Authorized Skill Stand In team members may remotely access the dedicated work environment for account management, operational support, and permitted project-related activities.

## Ongoing Compensation

After onboarding, compensation follows a revenue-sharing model. The QA Engineer receives 30% of the applicable income generated through their assigned accounts, projects, or sub-team, while Skill Stand In retains 70% for project coordination, operations, technical resources, and related services.

Additional payment terms and project-specific requirements are provided during onboarding.
