## About the Role

Skill Stand In is hiring experienced architecture, engineering, and construction professionals to review complex engineering tasks set in data-center projects.

This is a technical peer-review role. You will assess realistic design, permitting, and construction scenarios, repair gaps in task materials, and confirm that expected outputs and grading methods reflect professional practice. You will not design or build facilities.

## What You'll Do

- Review long-horizon tasks involving structural and civil design, geotechnical inputs, foundations and slabs, wind and seismic analysis, rooftop equipment loads, building codes, AHJ requirements, drawing packages, BIM coordination, and constructability.
- Check engineering assumptions, calculations, governing criteria, code interpretation, document precedence, and coordination across disciplines.
- Identify ambiguity, conflicting source documents, missing constraints, or unrealistic deliverables.
- Improve task prompts, supporting artifacts, acceptance criteria, rubrics, and programmatic checks.
- Produce clear technical findings and validate corrected tasks end to end.
- Work independently and asynchronously to deliver high-quality evaluations.

## What We're Looking For

- Professional experience in architecture, structural engineering, civil engineering, construction, or a closely related AEC discipline.
- Experience with data centers or other mission-critical facilities is strongly preferred.
- Familiarity with building codes, AHJ review, drawing packages, and BIM coordination.
- Ability to review calculations, load paths, foundations, and constructability constraints.
- Strong written communication and comfort documenting technical findings for non-specialist reviewers.
- Experience with peer review, QA/QC, or permitting support is a plus.
