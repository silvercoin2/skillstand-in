## About the Role

Skill Stand In partners with leading AI organizations to improve the quality and reliability of artificial intelligence systems.

We are looking for experts who can evaluate AI-generated responses, score outputs against professional standards, and provide structured feedback that helps AI models become more accurate and useful.

## What You'll Do

- Review AI-generated responses for accuracy, relevance, completeness, and quality in your field.
- Compare outputs against expert-level expectations and flag errors, missing context, and weak reasoning.
- Score tasks, files, and scoresheets using project rubrics, then explain discrepancies.
- Provide structured feedback that improves clarity, depth, and usefulness of model outputs.
- Identify inconsistencies across related examples and recommend how evaluation criteria should change.
- Work independently and asynchronously to deliver high-quality evaluations.

## What We're Looking For

- Strong expertise in your professional domain.
- Excellent analytical and written communication skills.
- Ability to evaluate complex information critically and explain why an answer is right or wrong.
- Experience with research, technical writing, education, or quality review preferred.
- AI experience is helpful but not required.
