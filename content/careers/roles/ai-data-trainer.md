## About the Role

Skill Stand In partners with leading AI organizations to improve the quality and reliability of artificial intelligence systems.

We are looking for experts who can evaluate AI-generated responses, create high-quality training data, and provide structured feedback that helps AI models become more accurate and useful.

## What You'll Do

- Review AI-generated content for accuracy, relevance, and quality.
- Create evaluation criteria and detailed feedback guidelines.
- Identify errors, missing information, reasoning issues, and inconsistencies.
- Develop realistic scenarios and benchmark tasks for AI systems.
- Compare AI outputs against expert-level expectations.
- Provide structured feedback to improve AI model performance.

## What We're Looking For

- Strong expertise in your professional domain.
- Excellent analytical and written communication skills.
- Ability to evaluate complex information critically.
- Experience with research, technical writing, education, or quality review preferred.
- AI experience is helpful but not required.
